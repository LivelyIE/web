# PTSB Increases Rates By Up To 0.35%

## 24 Oct 2026


PTSB increases its fixed rates by up to 0.35%. The new rates are effective from 22 October 2026.

Buyers with an existing loan offer need to draw down by 19 January 2027 to avail
of the previous rates.
